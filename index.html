Welcome to the Arcade! 🚀

Ready to level up your math skills? This is your ultimate math playground where you can practice, challenge yourself, and master math concepts while having fun playing interactive games. Select your track below to start playing!

⚠️ Arcade Pro-Tip & Progress Notice

These games are created for single-session play, meaning your progress is not saved automatically. If you refresh, close, or leave the page, your game will restart from the beginning!

📸 Need proof of completion for class? Every game now has a name field at the start and a one-click "Save Screenshot" button at the end — enter your name, and save proof of completion for your teacher when you finish!
